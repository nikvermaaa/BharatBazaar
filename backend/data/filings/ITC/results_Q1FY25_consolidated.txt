ITC LIMITED
FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2024 (Q1 FY25)
SEGMENT REPORTING AND CONSOLIDATED RESULTS

1. Financial Overview:
- Gross Revenue for the quarter was ₹18,077 crore, up 7.3% YoY.
- Revenue excluding Agri-Business grew 6.0% YoY.
- Total Profit Before Tax (PBT) was ₹6,520 crore.
- Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ₹4,917 crore.
- Earnings Per Share for the quarter was ₹4.01.

2. Segmental Revenue & Profit:
- FMCG - Cigarettes: Gross revenue grew 7.0% YoY to ₹7,918 crore; Segment PBIT grew 6.5% YoY to ₹4,960 crore with resilient volume growth and portfolio premiumisation.
- FMCG - Others (Staples, Biscuits, Personal Care): Revenue grew 6.3% YoY to ₹5,491 crore with segment EBITDA margin expanding 25 bps YoY.
- Hotels Business: Revenue stood at ₹700 crore, up 10.9% YoY on high base; Segment PBIT up 17.0% YoY on strong RevPAR and banquet demand.
- Agri Business: Revenue stood at ₹6,973 crore, up 22.2% YoY driven by value-added agri products, leaf tobacco exports, and coffee.
- Paperboards, Paper and Packaging: Revenue was ₹1,977 crore, impacted by cheap imports from China and low global pulp prices.

3. Strategic Developments:
- Scheme of Arrangement for demerger of the Hotels Business into ITC Hotels Limited was approved by the requisite majority of shareholders and is advancing towards regulatory sanction.
