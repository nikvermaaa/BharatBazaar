RELIANCE INDUSTRIES LIMITED
FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2024 (Q1 FY25)
CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

1. Financial Highlights:
- Gross Revenue for the quarter was ₹2,57,823 crore (US$ 30.9 billion), higher by 11.5% YoY, supported by continuing growth momentum in consumer businesses (Jio and Retail) and higher volume in Upstream business.
- Consolidated EBITDA increased to ₹42,748 crore (US$ 5.1 billion), up 2.0% YoY, driven by strong performance across digital services, retail, and upstream oil & gas.
- Net Profit (Profit After Tax before exceptional items) for the quarter stood at ₹17,445 crore (US$ 2.1 billion), compared to ₹18,258 crore in Q1 FY24, impacted by lower transportation fuel crack margins in O2C.
- Capital Expenditure for the quarter ended June 30, 2024 was ₹28,785 crore (US$ 3.5 billion).

2. Segment Performance:
A. Jio Platforms Limited (Digital Services):
- Gross Revenue was ₹34,548 crore, up 12.8% YoY.
- EBITDA was ₹14,646 crore, up 11.6% YoY with an EBITDA margin of 50.1%.
- Net Profit was ₹5,693 crore, up 11.7% YoY.
- Total customer subscriber base reached 489.7 million with 5G subscriber additions exceeding 130 million. ARPU for the quarter was ₹181.7 per subscriber per month.

B. Reliance Retail Ventures Limited:
- Gross Revenue stood at ₹75,615 crore, up 8.1% YoY, despite impact of election heatwaves and reduced footfalls in fashion & lifestyle.
- EBITDA reached ₹5,664 crore, up 10.5% YoY with an EBITDA margin of 8.5% on net revenue.
- Total operational footprint expanded to 18,918 retail stores across India with 79.7 million sq. ft. area.

C. Oil to Chemicals (O2C):
- Segment Revenue was ₹1,57,133 crore, up 18.1% YoY primarily on account of higher crude oil prices and volumes.
- EBITDA was ₹13,093 crore, down 14.3% YoY, due to a sharp 30% contraction in global transportation fuel cracks and petrochemical downstream deltas.

D. Oil and Gas (Exploration & Production):
- Segment Revenue was ₹6,179 crore, up 33.4% YoY.
- Segment EBITDA was ₹5,210 crore, up 29.8% YoY, supported by sustained volume of ~30 MMSCMD gas production from the deepwater KG D6 block.

3. Accounting Basis & Audit:
All figures reported above are on a CONSOLIDATED basis in accordance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS). The results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting on July 19, 2024.
