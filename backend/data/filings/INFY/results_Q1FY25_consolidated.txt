INFOSYS LIMITED
FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2024 (Q1 FY25)
CONSOLIDATED STATEMENT OF PROFIT AND LOSS

1. Financial Highlights:
- Revenues in Indian Rupee terms reached ₹39,315 crore, registering growth of 3.6% YoY and 2.1% QoQ.
- Revenues in constant currency (CC) grew 2.5% YoY and 2.4% QoQ.
- Operating Profit stood at ₹8,288 crore, reflecting an operating margin of 21.1%, an expansion of 30 basis points QoQ.
- Net Profit for the quarter was ₹6,368 crore, an increase of 7.1% YoY.
- Basic EPS was ₹15.38, up 7.3% YoY.
- Free Cash Flow for the quarter stood at ₹9,161 crore ($1.1 billion), up 115.5% YoY.

2. FY25 Guidance Revision:
- Full year FY25 revenue guidance in constant currency was raised to 3.0% - 4.0% (revised upwards from 1.0% - 3.0% given previously).
- Operating margin guidance was maintained at 20.0% - 22.0% for the full fiscal year FY25.

3. Deal Wins:
- Large deal TCV (Total Contract Value) was robust at $4.1 billion across 34 deals, with 57% net new contracts.

4. Headcount & Attrition:
- Total employee base stood at 315,332 as of June 30, 2024.
- Voluntary attrition for IT services on LTM basis was 12.7%, down from 17.3% in Q1 FY24.
