TATA CONSULTANCY SERVICES LIMITED (TCS)
FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2024 (Q1 FY25)
CONSOLIDATED REVENUE AND SEGMENT PERFORMANCE

1. Financial Highlights:
- Revenue from operations stood at ₹62,613 crore, representing a growth of 5.4% YoY in rupee terms and 4.4% YoY in constant currency (CC).
- Operating Margin (EBIT Margin) expanded by 150 basis points YoY to 24.7%, driven by disciplined execution and operational efficiencies.
- Net Profit (PAT) for the quarter rose 8.7% YoY to ₹12,040 crore, with a net margin of 19.2%.
- Earnings Per Share (EPS) for the quarter was ₹33.29.
- Cash conversion remained strong with operating cash flow at 102.6% of net profit.

2. Segment & Industry Highlights:
- BFSI (Banking, Financial Services and Insurance): Declined 0.3% YoY in CC, showing sequential stabilization.
- Manufacturing: Grew 9.4% YoY in constant currency.
- Energy, Resources and Utilities: Grew 5.7% YoY in CC.
- Life Sciences & Healthcare: Grew 4.0% YoY in CC.
- Regional Markets: India grew strongly at 61.8% YoY driven by the execution of the BSNL 4G/5G deployment contract.

3. Deal Wins & Order Book:
- Total Contract Value (TCV) for Q1 FY25 stood at $8.3 billion.
- North America TCV was $4.6 billion; BFSI TCV was $3.0 billion; Consumer Business TCV was $1.1 billion.

4. Human Resources & Talent:
- Total employee headcount stood at 606,998 as of June 30, 2024, a net addition of 5,452 employees during the quarter.
- IT services LTM (Last Twelve Months) attrition moderated further to 12.1%.

5. Dividend:
- The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹10.00 per equity share of ₹1 face value.
