HDFC BANK LIMITED
FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE FIRST QUARTER ENDED JUNE 30, 2024 (Q1 FY25)
CONSOLIDATED AND STANDALONE HIGHLIGHTS

1. Key Financial Figures:
- Consolidated Net Profit for the quarter ended June 30, 2024 was ₹16,475 crore.
- Net Interest Income (NII) for the quarter grew to ₹29,837 crore from ₹23,599 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2023.
- Core Net Interest Margin (NIM) was 3.47% on total assets, and 3.66% based on interest earning assets.
- Provisions and contingencies were ₹2,602 crore for the quarter.
- Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) under Basel III guidelines stood at 19.33% against regulatory requirement of 11.7%.

2. Asset Quality:
- Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) stood at 1.33% of gross advances as on June 30, 2024, compared to 1.41% as on June 30, 2023.
- Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) stood at 0.39% of net advances.

3. Balance Sheet Scale:
- Total Gross Advances amounted to ₹24,87,000 crore, up 52.6% YoY reflecting post-merger integration of HDFC Limited.
- Total Deposits stood at ₹23,79,000 crore, up 24.4% YoY.
- CASA deposits were ₹8,63,600 crore, with CASA ratio standing at 36.3%.
